## 🌟 About the Repository  
This repository is a comprehensive collection of tools, platforms, and resources designed to aid cybersecurity professionals and enthusiasts in tasks such as threat intelligence, digital forensics, and network defense. Whether you're investigating suspicious activities or securing your organization's assets, these tools can help you achieve your goals efficiently.

---

## 🛠️ Featured Online Threat Intelligence Platforms

### 1. [MISP (Malware Information Sharing Platform)](https://www.misp-project.org/)  
MISP is an open-source threat intelligence platform for sharing and correlating Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) from malware, targeted attacks, and more. It enhances collaboration within cybersecurity communities while supporting automation.

---

### 2. [AlienVault Open Threat Exchange (OTX)](https://otx.alienvault.com/)  
AlienVault OTX is a community-powered platform offering global threat intelligence. It provides real-time insights into malicious indicators, enabling organizations to detect and mitigate threats proactively.

---

### 3. [Pulsedive Threat Intelligence Platform](https://pulsedive.com/dashboard/soc)  
Pulsedive delivers a threat intelligence dashboard for analyzing malicious activities. It combines multiple data sources into actionable insights, making it ideal for SOC analysts and threat hunters.

---

### 4. [Yeti Platform](https://yeti-platform.io/)  
Yeti is an open-source threat intelligence platform designed for collecting, analyzing, and sharing cyber threat data. It helps connect relationships between IOCs, TTPs, and threat actors to create actionable intelligence.

---

### 5. [Cortex by TheHive Project](https://www.cortex.io/)  
Cortex automates the analysis of observables and actions, supporting seamless integration with TheHive for incident response and threat intelligence workflows.

---
